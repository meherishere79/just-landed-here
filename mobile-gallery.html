Twenty years in Maninagar

We didn't set out to become a big shop. Just an honest one.

Mobile Gallery started as a neighbourhood mobile counter, built on advice you could trust and prices that didn't need bargaining. Two decades later, the same people who bought their first phone here now bring their whole household's electronics needs to us — from the AC in the bedroom to the geyser in the bathroom.

No call centre, no chatbot. Just a shop on Maninagar Char Rasta, and a family that's been picking up the phone since 2005.
